Real chemistry, played as a game. The molgang glossary tracks every player-facing concept side by side with its quantum-chem-engineering source — one shelf for game vocabulary, one for the textbook terms that back it.

Game vocabulary

Currencies, age bands, factions — terms that exist only inside molgang's economy.

  • Age bands: Player-progression tiers (8-10, 11-13, 14-17, 18+) gating quests, mechanics, and chemistry depth.
  • Atom economy: Green-chemistry metric measuring the fraction of reactant atoms that end up in the desired product rather than waste.
  • Carbon credit: Tradeable permit representing the right to emit one tonne of CO₂ equivalent, earned by operating below emissions caps.
  • MOLCO2: In-game carbon-credit currency awarded for sequestration / efficient reactions.
  • Quests: Structured missions that guide player progression through synthesis, engineering, and sustainability challenges.

Quantum chemical engineering

Real-world thermodynamics + transport phenomena — IUPAC source, quantum-mechanically grounded.

  • Boltzmann distribution: Probability distribution giving the relative populations of quantum states as an exponential function of their energy and temperature.
  • Chemical potential: Partial molar Gibbs free energy — the thermodynamic force that drives matter to flow between phases or react.
  • Damköhler number (Da): Dimensionless ratio of reaction rate to transport rate — tells you whether kinetics or mixing limits a reactor.
  • Fugacity: Effective partial pressure of a real gas — replaces ideal-gas pressure in chemical-equilibrium expressions.
  • Graph test: smoke test for lightrag write hook
