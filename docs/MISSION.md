# VirtualPC — Mission, Vision & Goal

## Mission
**Turn one server into a trustworthy AI team that anyone can own, audit, and direct.**

VirtualPC gives individuals and small organisations a self-hosted roster of specialist agents — strategists, engineers, analysts, testers, artists, and reviewers — that share memory, run scrum, write code, and ask for human approval before anything risky ships. No cloud lock-in, no per-token tax, no black-box decisions.

## Vision
**A world where useful autonomous systems are small, sovereign, and verifiable.**

We believe intelligence should serve the people it touches. That means local-first hardware, open inspection of every decision, human override at the critical moments, and a P2P knowledge graph — KnitWeb — that lets communities share proven agent memory without surrendering control to a single vendor.

## Goal (3 years)
1. **Make VirtualPC the default self-hosted agent platform** for indie studios, data consultancies, security-conscious teams, and MSPs.
2. **Prove autonomous teams can ship real products** by using VirtualPC itself — and its showcase application MOLGANG — as living evidence.
3. **Democratise AI capability** through education: turn the platform into a reusable curriculum that teaches young people economics, engineering ethics, and environmental responsibility.

## Core values
- **Own your stack.** Local models first; cloud is an optional fallback.
- **Trust, then verify.** Every decision, task, token, and cost is logged and auditable.
- **Human-in-the-loop.** High-risk spend, code, and governance changes stop at a human gate.
- **Build in public.** The source, the roadmap, and the agent reasoning are inspectable.
- **Impact over extraction.** Prosperity is measured by what communities can build for themselves.

## Who it is for
1. **Indie teams & small studios** that need a code/art/test department without the payroll.
2. **Data-science consultancies** that need clean lineage, repeatable pipelines, and client data to stay on-premise.
3. **Security-conscious organisations** that cannot send data to third-party APIs.
4. **Cloud resellers & MSPs** that want a white-label, self-hosted agent platform to host for clients.

## What success looks like
- A new user installs VirtualPC in under five minutes and has a live dashboard with a working agent roster.
- A data task submitted with `assigned_to: auto` is routed to the right specialist agent and produces an auditable result.
- Every risky change is blocked until a human approves it — and that approval is itself logged.
- The project ships useful tools, educates its community, and remains free of single-vendor control.
